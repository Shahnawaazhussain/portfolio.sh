# Case study checklist

## CampusSpace
- Overview and context
- Problem statement (only what you can substantiate)
- Product goals and constraints
- Role definition: students, faculty, administrators
- Workflow brainstorming and rationale
- Information architecture and role-specific user flows
- Branding and visual direction
- Primary screens personally designed by you
- AI-assisted implementation: explain Claude's contribution accurately
- Screens you reviewed or refined
- Final product walkthrough
- Challenges, learnings and future improvements

Be precise about authorship: distinguish screens you designed, screens implemented with AI assistance, and screens you personally reviewed or refined. Do not claim research, testing, metrics or outcomes without evidence.

## Quiz Master
Overview, audience/problem, information architecture, quiz flow, wireframes, high-fidelity UI, interaction states, prototype and learnings.

## MovieFlix
Overview, feature scope, search/watchlist flows, responsive UI decisions, React/Tailwind/TMDB/localStorage implementation, challenges and learnings.
